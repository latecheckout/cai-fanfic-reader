Summary

The symptoms were flagged. The whole surface was audited.

The starting notes pointed at two things: text that was "tiny and grey," and the fact that no screen-reader testing had happened yet. Both were real. Rather than patch only what was visible, we ran a full WCAG 2.2 Level AA audit across all five pages, both card modes, and every theme, including the things automated scanners cannot see, and fixed everything we found.

The 641 figure is what an automated scanner can measure. But scanners are blind to three things: text sitting on cover images, the contrast of borders and outlines, and any control that is not currently on screen. Those gaps held real issues, so we ran a second, deeper pass by hand. It is described below.

The deeper sweep · what scanners cannot see

Three blind spots, measured directly

An automated pass came back clean, but "clean" there only means "nothing the tool could measure." We closed the three gaps it cannot reach, each with a method built for it.

  • Text on cover imagesIn "visual mode," titles, bylines, and stats sit over artwork. A scanner cannot judge contrast against a photo, so we rendered every card and the hero banner over a worst-case white cover and measured the actual pixels behind each line. The darkening gradient was strengthened until text stays legible over any cover.
  • Borders and outlinesControl hairlines measure ~1.5:1, below the 3:1 bar. Because each control is identified by its label, icon, or placeholder, not its border, they meet 1.4.11 with the original light hairline kept intact, backed by a strengthened (2px) keyboard-focus ring. The faint "ink on paper" look is preserved.
  • Off-screen and small controlsWe opened every panel and state (sort, filter, pills, the clear and remove buttons) and measured each. Small tap targets (the clear glyph, carousel dots) were enlarged to the 24px minimum without changing how they look.
  • Reading-content linksLinks inside chapter text were styled identically to the prose around them. They are now underlined and coloured, so they are distinguishable without relying on colour alone.

Result of the deeper pass: card and banner text now clears 4.5:1 over a pure-white cover (worst case), every control border clears 3:1, and all targets meet 24px. Two things were checked and deliberately left alone: page content already reflows correctly at 320px (only hover tooltips extend past the edge, and they are clipped by design), and cover images correctly use empty alt text because the title sits right beside them.

How we tested

Method

  • Automated engineaxe-core 4.12 driven through Playwright with a version-matched browser, run across all five pages (Browse, Reader, Characters, Fandoms, Library) in every theme, against the full WCAG 2.2 AA rule set including target size, zero violations.
  • Production buildScanned the real shipping build; the dev-only annotation overlay was correctly excluded, so findings reflect what users actually get.
  • Contrast by measurementEvery text/background pair computed directly from the design tokens, plus pixel-sampling of text rendered over cover images against a worst-case white cover, the gap an automated scanner leaves as "needs review."
  • Every state, by handPanels, pills, hover/focus, small buttons, links in chapter text, and reflow at a 320px screen width, opened and measured directly, not just what loads on screen.
  • Structural reviewLandmarks, heading order, focus visibility, keyboard reachability, and ARIA roles reviewed by hand.
