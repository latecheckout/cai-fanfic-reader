:::chapter Letter One: P to M (Undated)

*I don't know if this is going to reach anyone. Ekko built the thing and he says it works but he also said the last five things worked and three of them caught fire, so.*

*You don't know me. I don't know you. I found your name in a folded piece of paper that came out of the hextech resonator and that's as much explanation as I have.*

*My name starts with P. I'm sixteen (I think). I live in a city that's mostly underground and sometimes above. I have blue hair which I did myself and a rabbit called Dr. Snuggles who is not a comfort rabbit I just liked the name.*

*If you get this: hi. Tell me something true.*

*— P*

*Letter One: M to P (Hawkins, Indiana, 1987)*

*Okay so I've been staring at this letter for three days trying to decide if it's real or another thing the Upside Down is doing, and I've decided it's real because the handwriting smells like machine oil and there's no Upside Down smell (you'd know it if you'd smelled it, it's like rot and electricity).*

*My name is Max. I'm fifteen. I have red hair which I did not do myself, it came like this.*

*I've been in the hospital. I don't want to explain why. I'm better now except for the not entirely being able to see out of my left eye thing, which the doctors say is probably temporary but they've been saying that for eight months.*

*Something true: I like skateboarding better than anything else I've ever done and I haven't been able to do it since the hospital thing, and I'm more angry about that than I'm angry about the eye.*

*Tell me something true back.*

*— M*

:::end-chapter

:::chapter Letter Seven: P to M

*I've been thinking about what you said. About how the Upside Down is a place with no color.*

*The undercity isn't colorless but it's dim. When I was small I used to climb up to the Topside just to see what colors looked like with actual light behind them. My sister used to come with me. We'd sit on the edge and look at the bridges and the sky and the towers and it felt like a whole other world. Because it was. It was literally a whole other world with its own gravity and everything.*

*She's gone now. Something happened. I'm not— I'm not ready to explain what.*

*But I think about those times a lot. The two of us up there with the light.*

*I have a Jinx now. That's my other name. I call myself that because it felt true at a time when true felt important. I don't know if I still like it. I might not need it anymore.*

*I've been drawing again. I draw the things I can't say and then sometimes later I can say them.*

*I'm drawing your city from how you described it. The flat roads. The corn. A mall. I've never been to a mall. Is it what people say it is.*

*— P (Powder)*

---

*Letter Seven: M to P (Powder)*

*Powder. Okay. Hi.*

*Malls are not what people say they are but they're also exactly what people say they are. It's fluorescent lighting and the smell of pretzels and teenagers sitting outside stores because they don't have anywhere better to be. I used to think it was stupid. Now I kind of love it, I think because it's just — normal. It's just a normal thing. After everything, a pretzel stand is kind of amazing.*

*I want to hear about your sister. Only when you're ready.*

*I have a Mike, which is a person who loves me and makes it complicated, and a Lucas, who is my best friend and always right about things even when I don't want him to be. And El, who I think you would understand.*

*Drawing sounds right. I used to write in my journal. During the hospital thing I stopped. The hand was—anyway.*

*I'm going to try this week. Writing something down that's true.*

*— Max*

:::end-chapter
